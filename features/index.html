Features

Discover the key features of Bloxlink, the most complete solution to manage Roblox from Discord.

Roblox Verification

Verify your members and sync their Roblox data to Discord roles automatically.

Group Roles

Automatically assign Discord roles based on Roblox group membership and rank.

Server Lock

Restrict your Discord server to only verified Roblox users or specific group members.

Showcase Profile

Show your Roblox profile on Discord with rich presence and profile cards.

Custom Binds

Create custom role binds based on Roblox assets, badges, gamepasses, and more.

Auto-Update

Automatically update member roles and nicknames when their Roblox data changes.
